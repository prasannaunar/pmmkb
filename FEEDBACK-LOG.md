## Quarterly Review Cycle (One Framework Per Week)

Per PLAN.md Phase 5.1: review one framework per week, roughly 7 weeks per quarter, asking:
- Has the framework held up to use? Is it still relevant?
- Are there new examples or case studies to add?
- Have pitfalls evolved?
