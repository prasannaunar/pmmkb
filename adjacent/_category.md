# Adjacent Disciplines

These entries come from neighbouring disciplines rather than from product marketing practice. A PMM rarely runs them as a method of their own, and the decision they feed belongs to someone else: portfolio and growth choices sit with the CEO, CFO or corporate strategy, and persuasion research belongs to behavioural science. PMMs still meet them constantly, in board decks, planning cycles and messaging reviews, so each entry says plainly what PMM contributes under "PMM's role" instead of "Ownership". They are not counted in the knowledge base's headline entry total.

A SWOT analysis is deliberately not an entry here. Its origin is disputed and it is widely criticised for producing lists rather than decisions; Porter's Five Forces and the BCG matrix do the same diagnostic work more rigorously.
