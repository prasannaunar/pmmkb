A working companion to the [Competitive Intelligence & Positioning Update Framework](../frameworks/07-strategy-planning.md) (Category 7). This was listed in [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) Phase 2.4 alongside the other three operational templates; it lives here because the audit checklist and quarterly template above both reference it directly.

## Monthly Scan Checklist

Run monthly, per competitor. Time-box to 30-45 minutes per competitor.

- [ ] Website and pricing page reviewed for changes
- [ ] Feature/changelog or release notes reviewed
- [ ] Recent press, funding, or leadership announcements checked
- [ ] G2/Capterra reviews scanned for new themes (both praise and complaints)
- [ ] Sales team asked: "Did you hear this competitor's name in a deal this month? What came up?"

## Competitive Matrix Update Schedule

- **Monthly:** scan checklist above, all tracked competitors
- **Quarterly:** deep-dive template above, top 1-2 competitors; refresh the [Perceptual Map](../frameworks/03-competitive-strategy.md) or [Bowman's Strategic Clock](../frameworks/03-competitive-strategy.md) position if it has moved
- **Ad hoc:** any escalation trigger below, regardless of schedule

## When to Escalate

Escalate outside the normal cadence (notify sales leadership and update the Battlecard within 48 hours) when a competitor:

- Changes pricing or packaging in a way that affects active deals
- Announces a feature that closes a differentiation gap named in current messaging
- Is named in three or more deals in a single week where it wasn't previously a factor
- Raises a funding round that materially changes their ability to compete on price or headcount

---

**Success metric for this tracker:** every tracked competitor has a monthly scan row with no blank cells; at least one quarterly deep-dive exists per top competitor per quarter; no escalation trigger goes more than 48 hours without a Battlecard update.
