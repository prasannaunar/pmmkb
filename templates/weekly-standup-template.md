# Weekly Standup Briefing Template

A 5-minute briefing format for PMM teams to stay anchored to frameworks during weekly standup, rather than letting framework use become a quarterly-only exercise. Fill in the three questions below before each standup; keep answers to one or two lines each.

---

## Week of: _____________

### 1. What framework should we apply this week?

Name the specific framework or methodology and the trigger that surfaced it (see [BY-SITUATION.md](../BY-SITUATION.md) if you're not sure which one fits).

> Example: "Win rate against Competitor X dropped two weeks running; running the Perceptual Map this week to check whether they've repositioned."

### 2. What framework are we waiting to use until we have data?

Name the framework and the specific data gap blocking it, plus who owns closing that gap.

> Example: "Want to re-run the Sean Ellis 40% Test on the new segment, but need 30+ responses from users active in the last two weeks; waiting on lifecycle marketing to trigger the survey."

### 3. What framework-driven deliverable is due?

Name the deliverable, the framework behind it, and the due date.

> Example: "Updated Battlecard for Competitor Y due Friday, feeding this week's [Competitive Intelligence](../frameworks/07-strategy-planning.md) scan."
