# Sources Explored

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-19 (Product Compass template list reviewed; three entries built; the (R)ICE verdict in the 2026-10-15 review corrected)

Every external source reviewed for candidate entries, with the verdict on each item. Use it to avoid re-reviewing a source, and to see why something was left out. Candidates and build status are tracked in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) ("Candidate review: Product Compass template list" and "Candidate review: external template and resource lists"); citations for built entries live in each entry's `**Sources:**` block, with judgement calls recorded in [SOURCE-VALIDATION-LOG.md](SOURCE-VALIDATION-LOG.md).

**How items were judged:** CLAUDE.md's curation bar ("proven, replicable", "no duplicate concepts under different names") and the PMM vs GTM inclusion test ("who runs this"). Since 2026-10-15, items that fail the inclusion test but are proven tools PMMs regularly feed or apply can go into [`adjacent/`](adjacent/) instead (see CLAUDE.md, "The `adjacent/` area").

**Verdict key:** **Built** (now an entry) · **Adjacent** (built in `adjacent/`) · **Covered** (an existing entry already does the job) · **Out of scope** (owned by another function, or an execution template rather than a framework) · **Fails bar** (not proven or replicable, single practitioner, or no defensible source) · **Not reviewed** (could not be accessed).

## 2026-10-19 review

### 1. Paweł Huryn, "30+ Free Product Management Templates"

[The Product Compass](https://www.productcompass.pm/p/free-product-management-templates) (updated August 2026). Most downloadable files now unlock with a free Substack sign-up. The Google Docs and Sheets links for the discovery, prioritisation and roadmap items were opened through their export links. The Figma kit page returned 403, so its list of 27 templates was read from search-engine indexes. The premium Notion templates are paid. Per the owner, sources that could not be accessed are left out rather than worked around.

The JTBD Opportunity Score calculation sheet led to **Outcome-Driven Innovation (ODI)** (**Built**, Category 1), written from Tony Ulwick's own published method rather than the sheet. The Value Curve item led to **Strategy Canvas and ERRC Grid** (**Built**, Category 3), and the Hypothesis Prioritization Canvas, Test Card and Learning Card items led to **Assumptions Mapping** (**Built**, Category 4), with David J. Bland's *Testing Business Ideas* as the best-documented version. Assumptions Mapping is a core entry rather than adjacent because a PMM can run the whole process for a launch or segment entry and produce the map, the Test Cards and the Learning Cards.

### 2. Paweł Huryn, The Product Management Frameworks Compendium

Lists 35 product management frameworks in eight groups. **Paywalled after the first group**; the archived copy could not be reached from the review environment, and the owner chose to leave it as is rather than subscribe. The hidden groups are decision-making (4), stakeholder management (2), product metrics (3), product strategy (7), strategy-related artefacts (5), agile (3) and other (2); page anchors show a SWOT section among them.

The nine topic databases (examples and inspiration, research, competitive intel, personas and segmentation, positioning and messaging, pricing and packaging, go-to-market and launches, sales enablement, operations) held 85 links; the course, career, leaders, newsletter and podcast databases were not mined.
