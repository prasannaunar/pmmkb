# Win/loss interview guide

**Interviewer:** [neutral to the deal; not the rep who worked it]
**Deal:** [account] | **Outcome:** Won / Lost / No decision | **Closed:** [date]
**Buyer role:** [economic / technical / end user] | **Interview date:** [within 30 to 90 days of close]

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

"Thanks for the time. I am not on the sales team and nothing you say affects
your account or pricing. I am trying to understand how the decision actually
happened, including the parts that did not go well for us. There are no wrong
answers."

---

## Core questions (30 to 40 minutes)

1. What problem were you trying to solve? What made you start looking when you did?
2. What options did you evaluate? How did you first hear about each one?
3. Who else was involved in the decision, and what did each of them care about?
4. What was the primary reason you chose [us / them]?
5. What almost stopped the deal happening at all?
6. What surprised you about our product compared with the alternatives?
7. Looking back, what could we have shown or explained earlier that would have changed your timeline or your decision?

**Probes to use, not to skip:**
- On price: "What were you comparing that price against, and what did you expect to get for it?"
- On a competitor: "What did they show you that we did not?"
- On timing: "What would have had to be true for you to decide a quarter earlier?"

---

## Close

"Anything I should have asked and did not?"
