# Framework Selector

Pick the right framework in under 60 seconds. Answer three questions: what is your business goal, how much time do you have, and what data do you already hold? The table below returns a starting framework, one or two follow-ups, and a link to the full sequence.

**How this fits with other navigation tools:** [decision-trees.md](decision-trees.md) triages a symptom into a situation; [BY-SITUATION.md](BY-SITUATION.md) gives you the full ordered sequence once you know your situation. This tool sits between them: you know the goal and constraints but not which framework to open first.
